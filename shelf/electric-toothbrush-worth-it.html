The Verdict

Electric toothbrush vs manual: worth an extra $41 to $47 a year?

Short answerWorth it, narrowly. Powered brushes as a group beat manual ones on plaque and gum inflammation, but the largest body of evidence is on rotating brushes, not the sonic Suri we use. The size of the benefit for your actual teeth is unclear, and a manual brush used well also works.

What the evidence says

The Cochrane review of powered versus manual brushing included 56 trials; 51 trials with 4,624 people provided data. Compared with manual brushing, powered brushes as a group reduced plaque by about 11% at 1 to 3 months and about 21% after 3 months. Gingivitis (gum inflammation) fell by about 6% and about 11%. The review rates the evidence as moderate quality.

The same review says plainly that "the clinical importance of these findings remains unclear." The American Dental Association says both types of toothbrush can clean teeth effectively and thoroughly. So the honest framing is a measurable but modest edge.

By brush type, the review's abstract says: "The greatest body of evidence was for rotation oscillation brushes which demonstrated a statistically significant reduction in plaque and gingivitis at both time points." It lists 27 trials of rotating brushes and 10 of side-to-side brushes, but reports no separate result for side-to-side brushes. Whether sonic designs fall in that group is not stated in the abstract. So the pooled result for all powered brushes is positive, and the clearest by-type result is for rotating brushes. We cannot say from the review that sonic brushes like Suri match them.

Our pick: Suri

This part is experience, not evidence. We've tried them all and replaced them three or four times before moving to a sonic brush with Suri. Suri is the one we kept: it feels like the iPhone of toothbrushes, a very high-end product.

Design facts, from Suri's own site: the replacement heads are made from cornstarch with bristles made from castor oil, and used heads can be recycled free with a prepaid mailer. Neither was tested in the review.

What this does not tell you

  • The review does not test any specific brand, so we cannot say how Suri performs against manual in particular.
  • The review we read was published in 2014, with searches to January 2014. We did not check whether a later update exists.
  • Our cost model uses assumptions we chose, listed in the math below. Change them and the answer moves a little.
